# Players

Use these guides to install mods and protect your offline saves.

- [Installing a mod](/en/players/installing-a-mod)
- [Current D2RLoader documentation](https://d2rloader.net/docs.html)

Before you test an unfamiliar mod, make a separate backup of your character and shared stash saves.
Keep saves from different mods separate. Do not restore a save while its character is in a game.

Multiplayer, settings, and troubleshooting guides will be added after the initial navigation review.
